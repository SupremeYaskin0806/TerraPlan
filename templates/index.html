        Agricultural Knowledge Center

        Essential agronomy principles and smart farming techniques.

        Drip irrigation delivers water and nutrients directly to plant root zones, saving 40-60% of water compared to traditional flood irrigation while mitigating fungal diseases caused by standing water.

        Nitrogen (N) supports vegetative and leaf canopy growth, Phosphorus (P) accelerates root development and early flowering, and Potassium (K) enhances drought resistance, disease tolerance, and fruit size.

        Alternating heavy nitrogen consumers (like Maize or Cotton) with legumes (like Groundnut or Pulses) replenishes natural soil nitrogen through root-nodule rhizobia and breaks recurring insect life cycles.
